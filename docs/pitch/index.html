From boss drop to sale

  1. MVP boss drops a rare card
  2. World ID verify, mirrored onchain
  3. Server-signed EIP-712 voucher mints ERC-1155
  4. Listed in the Rare Market
  5. Buyer pays: 90% seller, 10% treasury
  6. Unverified bot: refused
  7. Second wallet, same human: refused
