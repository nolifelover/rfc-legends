# Thai Riverside Character Prompts

All seven assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The existing
moonlit khlong keyframe was used only as a mood, palette, and lighting reference.
Final mobile runtime files were resized with Lanczos filtering and encoded as
alpha WebP: trainer poses are 240×240; rooster variants are 280×280. Phaser
continues to display them at the established logical-stage sizes.

## `trainer-idle.webp`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent game character sprite, trainer idle pose, for a 1920×1080 Phaser stage; will display as a 400 px-tall character inside a 440×440 texture with origin centered at bottom.
Input image: mood and color reference only; do not copy the scene or its exact character.
Primary request: Create one original adult Thai male trainer, full body, standing in a friendly ready idle pose facing three-quarters right. He wears an ochre collarless Thai work shirt, deep indigo chong kraben trousers, a rust-red and ivory striped pha khao ma sash tied at the waist, and is barefoot. Warm, capable expression; black hair; clear Thai identity. He is a guardian/farmer, not a fighter.
Style/medium: polished collectible cartoon game character with restrained dark warm outlines and painterly layered cel shading; crisp silhouette at mobile thumbnail size; proportions charming but grounded, not chibi.
Lighting/mood: cool moonlit blue rim light from upper left, subtle warm amber lantern highlights from right.
Color palette: River Ink #183946, Indigo #283B63, Aged Teak #50372B, Lantern Gold #F2B45B, Rice Paper #F4E6C7; skin natural warm brown.
Composition/framing: square sprite canvas, full body completely visible, head near y=24%, both bare feet aligned at a shared baseline around y=91%, centered horizontally, generous transparent padding on all sides, no cast shadow beyond a tiny soft contact shadow.
Constraints: truly transparent background with clean alpha; exactly one character; no props, weapons, scenery, text, logo, watermark, border, glow box, blood, injury, aggression, photorealism, 3D render, anime, or existing IP. Keep hands and feet anatomically clean and readable. Original art inspired only by the reference mood.
```
